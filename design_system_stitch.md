# Dilegno Design System

## Brand Core
- **Name:** Dilegno
- **Tagline:** Vestindo Espaços
- **Mission:** Materializar y concretar ideas de decoración, diseño de interiores y ambientación para que los espacios cobren vida e identidad.
- **Vision:** Ser reconocidos como una marca que busca una relación auténtica y transparente con sus clientes para que nuestro asesoramiento y nuestras soluciones sean una distinción en la manera de hacer las cosas.
- **Target Audience:** Arquitectos y/o diseñadores de interiores que necesitan asesoramiento en la decoración y diseño de sus espacios.
- **Brand Personality (Archetypes):**
  - **The Creator:** Original, innovative, perfectionist, intuitive. Imagines and brings to reality.
  - **The Caregiver:** Charismatic, empathetic, generous. Aims to help others and works collaboratively.
- **Tone of Voice:** Suave, auténtico, cálido, coloquial (con un toque de humor), transparente, honesto, directo y confiable.

## Typography
- **Primary Typeface:** Source Sans 3 (Designed by Paul D. Hunt)
  - Characteristics: Sans serif, moderna, versátil, legible.
  - Weights: ultralight, light, regular, media, semibold, bold, ultrabold, black.
  - Usage: Headlines, body text, and highlights.

## Color Palette
The palette consists of high-contrast achromatics, a warm white, and a desaturated accent for sophistication.

1. **Sombra (Dark Acromatic)**
   - HEX: `#2E2D2C`
   - RGB: 46, 45, 44
   - CMYK: 0, 0, 0, 95

2. **Eucalipto (Desaturated Accent)**
   - HEX: `#B2C2B2`
   - RGB: 178, 194, 178
   - CMYK: 36, 16, 33, 0

3. **Lino (Warm White)**
   - HEX: `#F5F3E9`
   - RGB: 245, 243, 233
   - CMYK: 5, 4, 10, 0

4. **Lienzo (Light Acromatic / Gray)**
   - HEX: `#EBEBEB`
   - RGB: 235, 235, 235
   - CMYK: 0, 0, 0, 11

## Visual Identity & Logos
- **Isologo:** Plays with the ambiguity of the lowercase letter "n" and a graphic synthesis sharing morphological traits with a Nordic-style chair.
- **Primary Logotype:** Incorporates the isologo within the typography of "Dilegno" for a unique character.
- **Lockup:** "Dilegno" (Logotype) with "VESTINDO ESPAÇOS" (Tagline) below.
- **Seal/Badge:** A circular badge encapsulating the tagline ("MÓVEIS & DECORAÇÃO" / "VESTINDO ESPAÇOS") around the central "n" (chair) isologo. Used for cohesion and aesthetic reinforcement.

## Illustrations & Imagery
- **Illustrations:** Inspired by technical design blueprints, combining thin lines with blocks of color to create visual balance.
- **Photography/Mockups:** Features clean, minimalist environments, warm lighting, natural shadows, and organic textures (wood, fabrics, ceramics).
